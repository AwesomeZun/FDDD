Downloaded unmodified as top-andre-karwath.jpg for visual reference. No texture or mesh derived from the photograph. Procedural models are separately authored.

User rejected mosquito/bee appearance twice. Every candidate must retain fruit-fly proportions: compact thorax, broad short abdomen without a wasp waist or needle tip, short antennae with arista, brick-red compound eyes, broad rounded wings. The photograph shows resting wings over the abdomen; flight spread is a separate pose, not a different insect body.
