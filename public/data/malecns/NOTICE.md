Inclusion is Traced plus annotated non-tbc neurons with null status, including peripheral sensory neurons; unclassified Traced cells retained, glia/orphans/anchors/assign/unimportant excluded.

Measured soma/tosoma locations are anatomical samples, not synthetic FlyWire shapes and not full neurite reconstructions. All167122 neurons are computed and recorded regardless of visualization sample. Brain and VNC neurons share one real measured connectivity graph. Sensory superclass cells receive authored descriptor encoding; output reads annotated cb_motor and vnc_motor neurons. Predicted GABA/glutamate use negative central weights; all other/unknown transmitters positive. This sign model, normalized LIF parameters, motor-bin decoder, movement and rewards are authored simplifications, not validated physiology. No Vina-affinity generalization from previous FlyWire training is claimed. Old FlyWire bundles and records keep their original provenance/licenses and are not relicensed by this file.
